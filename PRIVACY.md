# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy — Floating Video Downloader

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-11-21

## 中文

Floating Video Downloader（以下简称“本扩展”）不收集、不上传、不出售任何用户数据。

- **不联网上传**：本扩展没有自己的服务器，不向开发者或任何第三方发送数据。
- **Cookie**：只有当你在受支持站点（抖音、B站、小红书、X/Twitter、Instagram、TikTok、新片场）主动点击“下载”时，本扩展才会读取该站点的 Cookie，并通过 Chrome Native Messaging 临时交给你本机安装的下载组件，用来下载你登录后有权观看的公开视频。下载组件处理完立即删除临时文件，Cookie 不离开你的电脑。
- **网页地址与标题**：点击下载时，当前视频页面的地址和标题会传给本机下载组件，用于下载和命名文件，不会发送到其他地方。
- **本地设置**：保存目录等设置只存在你电脑上的配置文件里。
- **无统计、无广告、无追踪**。

如有问题，请在 GitHub 提交 issue：https://github.com/dguan29-lgtm/yt-dlp-floating-downloader/issues

## English

Floating Video Downloader ("the extension") does not collect, transmit, or sell any user data.

- **No remote servers**: the extension has no backend and sends nothing to the developer or any third party.
- **Cookies**: only when you click "Download" on a supported site (Douyin, Bilibili, Xiaohongshu, X/Twitter, Instagram, TikTok, Xinpianchang), the extension reads that site's cookies and passes them, via Chrome Native Messaging, to the download component installed on your own computer, so it can fetch public videos you are allowed to view. Temporary files are deleted right after use; cookies never leave your computer.
- **Page URL and title**: the current video page URL and title are passed to the local component to download and name the file, and are not sent anywhere else.
- **Local settings**: settings such as the download folder are stored only in a local config file.
- **No analytics, ads, or tracking.**

Questions: https://github.com/dguan29-lgtm/yt-dlp-floating-downloader/issues
